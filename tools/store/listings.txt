=== en | English
SUMMARY: One-click Download button on every Reddit post: images, galleries, videos with sound, GIFs, Redgifs and Imgur. Bulk-download subreddits and profiles, follow sources and get new posts automatically. No tracking.
---
GrabPost adds an orange Download button to every Reddit post. One click saves the post's images, the whole gallery, or the video with its sound.

ONE CLICK ON EVERY POST
• Works in feeds, profiles, search results and single post pages, on new and old Reddit
• Images, galleries, GIFs, Redgifs and Imgur links
• Videos with sound: Reddit keeps the audio in a separate file; GrabPost finds it and merges it inside your browser
• Images in comments, a right-click Download entry, and Alt+Shift+D for the post in the middle of the screen

BULK DOWNLOAD
• Fast mode reads a subreddit, profile, multireddit or search straight from Reddit's data, without scrolling
• Skips what you already have and stops at posts you downloaded before
• Download your saved posts in one go (when logged in)
• Filter by media type and date range
• When Reddit asks for a break, GrabPost waits as long as asked and carries on

FOLLOW SUBREDDITS AND USERS
• Scheduled checks with desktop notifications, optional automatic download
• Filters per source: media type and keywords
• Up to 10 favorites checked as often as every 5 minutes
• Missed checks catch up a couple of minutes after the browser starts
• Export and import your list

FILES AND HISTORY
• Folder and file name templates: {subreddit}, {user}, {date}, {title} and more
• Duplicate detection by image content, not just the address
• Searchable history; posts you already have are marked on Reddit
• Works next to download managers such as IDM without losing your folder names
• Automatic backups, so a reinstall does not wipe your history

PRIVACY
No analytics, no tracking, no account, no servers of our own. GrabPost talks only to Reddit and the sites that host the media you download. Settings, follow list and history stay in your browser.

• 快速模式直接从 Reddit 数据读取子版块、个人主页、多版块或搜索结果，无需滚动
• 跳过已有的内容，遇到以前下载过的帖子就停下
• 一次下载你收藏的帖子（需登录）
• 按媒体类型和日期范围筛选
• Reddit 要求暂停时，GrabPost 会按要求的时间等待，然后继续

关注子版块和用户
• 定时检查，桌面通知，可选自动下载
• 每个来源单独筛选：媒体类型和关键词
• 最多 10 个收藏，最快每 5 分钟检查一次
• 错过的检查会在浏览器启动几分钟后自动补上
• 列表可导出和导入

文件和历史记录
• 文件夹和文件名模板：{subreddit}、{user}、{date}、{title} 等
• 按图片内容识别重复，而不只是看地址
• 可搜索的历史记录；已经拥有的帖子会在 Reddit 上标出
• 可与 IDM 等下载管理器共存，文件夹名称不会丢失
• 自动备份，重新安装也不会清空历史记录

隐私
无分析、无追踪、无需账号、没有我们自己的服务器。GrabPost 只与 Reddit 以及托管你所下载媒体的网站通信。设置、关注列表和历史记录都留在你的浏览器里。
